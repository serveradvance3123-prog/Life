Before you scroll further...

Take one deep breath.

How long has it been since someone asked how you truly feel...

and actually waited for the answer?

We spend our lives surrounded by people,

yet carry our heaviest silencies entirely alone.

Think about the words you swallowed.

The apologies you never received. The love you were too afraid to name.

Where did the version of you go...

the one that believed anything was possible?

Perhaps you are not falling behind.

Perhaps you are simply unlearning the expectations of a world that doesn't know your heart.

Every person passing you right now is fighting an invisible battle.

Just like you.

Your story isn't over.

Choose gentleness. Choose understanding.

A single act of kindness can rewrite someone's entire day.

Sometimes, it is enough just to stay.
